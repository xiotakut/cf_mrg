## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1: section_completeness

I will write every section required by the approved outline for the English empirical AI conference paper, including the material needed to complete each section's purpose. I will resolve unsupported or missing material before handing the manuscript to citation compliance; an unresolved material-gap placeholder cannot count as a completed section.

### D2: citation_density

I will attach identifiable support to factual claims, use the assigned sources accurately, and remove or flag claims that lack support rather than disguise the gap with tentative language. Any absence or novelty claim will be bounded by an actual documented literature search and will name and cite the nearest relevant prior work when available; I will not invent search provenance or a universal priority claim.

### D3: argument_blueprint_fidelity

I will follow the claim–evidence–reasoning chains in the supplied Argument Blueprint: each section's main point will match its assigned claim, its supporting evidence will come from the assigned material, and its explanation will show how that evidence answers the section's question. The blueprint will determine these chains, not assumptions drawn from the paper title or this contract.

### D4: total_word_count

I will target a 5,500-word main body and keep the completed body within ten percent of that target, or 4,950–6,050 words, while also respecting any separately stated author ceiling. I will measure the draft and report its actual count instead of estimating compliance from length on the page.

### D5: per_section_word_count

I will use the per-section allocations supplied in the Paper Configuration Record and keep each section within fifteen percent of its allocation. If a section is short or long, I will improve or tighten its supported argument before reporting any remaining deviation; I will not invent allocations before seeing the record.

### D6: paragraph_structure

I will make each paragraph perform a clear role with adequate evidential support and explanation, and connect adjacent sections so the argument progresses coherently. Paragraph length and form will serve the scientific content and the author's requirements rather than a mechanical paragraph template.

### D7: register_consistency

I will maintain direct, precise scientific English suitable for an empirical AI conference paper. The draft will use consistent terminology and a professional academic register, state supported findings clearly, and retain only qualifications needed to express the actual evidence without excessive defensiveness.
