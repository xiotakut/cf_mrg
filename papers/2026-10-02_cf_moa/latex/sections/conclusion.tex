\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluate seven medical question-answering configurations on a common full-v5
benchmark and improve every configuration on the complete R1, R2, and R3 development
strata through question-scoped support recomputation and original-model catalog
re-decision. Component controls distinguish these system gains from the variable effects
of calibration, repeated decisions, and answer-distribution policies. The subsequent
two-MedRAG CF-MoA experiment evaluates a broader composition and finds strong core gains,
followed by mixed incremental effects from rationale-conditioned verification. The
seven-system comparison, component studies, and six-arm follow-up answer complementary
questions; each retains its actual scope and computation. The results support reusable
terminal decision components with measured development benefits, while independent
confirmation of the final composition and candidate-pool replication remain future work.
