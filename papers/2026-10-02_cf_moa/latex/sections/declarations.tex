\section{Author and Availability Declarations}
\label{app:declarations}

\paragraph{Data and code availability.}
The manuscript source, figure-generation scripts, frozen result summaries, and experiment evidence indexes are maintained in the authors' private repository. Redistribution of source datasets must follow each original provider's terms; where redistribution is restricted, a release can provide identifiers, acquisition instructions, and derived membership or scoring metadata.

\paragraph{Author information.}
This generic conference draft uses anonymous author information. Author names, affiliations, contribution statements, funding information, and competing-interest disclosures remain to be completed by the authors before submission.

\paragraph{AI assistance.}
An artificial intelligence assistant was used to organize existing experiment records, draft and edit text, prepare LaTeX, generate plotting code, and check consistency against saved artifacts. All reported evaluation results, labels, and predictions come from saved experiment artifacts. The authors remain responsible for verifying the manuscript, disclosures, citations, and source-data permissions.
