\section*{Limitations}

The benchmark and component panels informed method development. Bootstrap intervals quantify uncertainty within these evaluated families; performance on unseen families requires a separate family-disjoint evaluation after final controller selection. System coverage comprises two MedRAG configurations for the final controller and seven configurations for the earlier support and comparison heads.

Category coverage is uneven: support removal contains 79 units and consequence calculation contains 13. Labels overlap, and a substantial part of the preservation subset comes from perturbation and retrieval-robustness tasks. Taxonomy assignments underwent structured review and source-specific checks; independent clinician agreement remains unmeasured. Reference labels and scoring rubrics inherit the properties of their source datasets.

Component performance depends on the supplied resources. The rule executor uses a 71-rule bank and extracted facts, diagnosis uses a finite public catalog, and the consequence agent was validated on a supplied physiological model and virtual profiles. The measured gains include contributions from candidate coverage and readable output. Isolating changes in latent clinical reasoning requires controlling these resources. Retrieval, formatting, and inference budgets differ across baselines; a complete resource-matched comparison remains open.

\section*{Ethical Considerations}
The study evaluates existing research datasets and simulated profiles. Patient-care use requires clinical validation beyond these evaluations. Dataset provenance and original access conditions must accompany redistribution. Evaluation artifacts include source task contracts, failures, and invalid outputs for inspecting behavior at the individual-input level. Author and data-availability declarations appear in Appendix~\ref{app:declarations}.
